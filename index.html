About Loam

Rooted in Dubai. Growing in Iraq.

Loam Real Estate was founded in Dubai in 2021, built on the entrepreneurial legacy of the Ajlan family and a track record across both the UAE and Iraq. Loam Real Estate IQ brings that experience home to Baghdad: an Emirati real estate marketing company representing a wide group of residential projects throughout the country.

The name says it. Loam is the richest soil, the ground where things take root. We work the same way, matching buyers with developments that hold their value and giving developers a sales partner with the discipline of the Dubai market.

    Market insight

    Why Baghdad, why now

    Iraq is in the middle of one of the region's largest housing drives. For buyers, that means more choice and better build quality than at any point in the last two decades. For investors, it means entering a market before it matures, not after.

    A note on due diligence

    We encourage every buyer to verify project licences, developer records and payment protections before committing. We will help you do it.

    1. 01

      Structural demand

      A young, growing population and a long-standing housing shortfall keep demand for quality homes well ahead of supply.

    2. 02

      National housing programme

      Government-backed new cities and large-scale residential projects around Baghdad are reshaping where and how the city grows.

    3. 03

      Regional developers arriving

      Established Gulf and Egyptian developers have entered Iraq, raising standards for design, delivery and after-sales.

    4. 04

      Prime districts

      Al-Mansour and its neighbouring districts remain Baghdad's most sought-after addresses, and our sales centre is based right there.
